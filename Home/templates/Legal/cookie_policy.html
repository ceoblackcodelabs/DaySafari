Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. This page explains the cookies used on daysafarisadventures.co.ke. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data generally.

2. {% trans "Third-party content" %}

Some pages load content from other providers, which may set their own cookies or collect technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests the content:

  • Google Maps on the contact page (embedded map).
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome / jsDelivr / Google-hosted libraries for fonts, icons and scripts.
  • Social and sharing links (Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube) if you click through to them.
  • Payment providers (for example Stripe or M-Pesa) on payment pages.

Please see those providers’ own privacy and cookie policies for details.

3. {% trans "Local storage" %}

The site briefly stores short notification messages (for example “message sent”) in your browser’s local storage so they can be shown after a page reload, then removes them. This data stays on your device.

4. {% trans "Managing cookies" %}

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge all provide this). If you block strictly necessary cookies, forms and sign-in will not work. You can change language at any time with the language selector at the top of the site.

5. {% trans "Changes" %}

If we add analytics or marketing cookies in future we will update this page and ask for your consent where required.
