FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Day Safaris Adventures specialize in?

We specialize in African wildlife safaris, tailor-made tours, beach holidays, mountain adventures, cultural experiences, airport transfers, hotel bookings, visa assistance, and international travel packages.

Where are you located?

Our head office is conveniently located at Nairobi Railways Museum, Nairobi, Kenya (00200), making it easy for clients to visit us for travel consultations and bookings.

What are your business hours?

We are open Monday to Sunday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Our customer support team is available throughout these hours to assist with bookings and travel inquiries.

How can I contact Day Safaris Adventures?

You can reach us via email at info@daysafarisadventures.com or call us on +254 759 379 600. off - office number is +254 734 962 965. You may also visit our office during business hours.

Can you help with visa processing?

Yes. We provide professional visa processing assistance, helping clients prepare documents, complete applications, and track their visa status for various destinations.

How do I book a safari?

Booking is easy. Simply fill out our online booking form, call our travel consultants, or send us an email. We'll help you choose the perfect package and customize your adventure to suit your budget and preferences.
